Why Line Forge

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Built for real kitchens by people who've worked them. Fast, offline-first, and purpose-built for the line.

⚡
Instant drag & drop
Pull equipment from the palette and drop it anywhere on the canvas. Snap-to-grid keeps everything aligned without manual math.
🔄
Rotate, resize, duplicate
Rotate any item and its dimensions swap automatically — width becomes height, exactly as equipment behaves in the real world.
📦
Export in one click
Export your layout as JSON or print it directly. Import any saved layout instantly to pick up where you left off.
📐
True-to-scale equipment
Every item in the palette is modeled at a consistent prototype scale — ranges, pans, prep tables, and cold rails all relate to each other correctly.
🔴
Collision detection
Overlapping items are highlighted with a dashed red outline so you can see crowded zones at a glance — without ever blocking placement.
🌐
Offline-first, no server
Runs entirely in the browser. No account, no cloud sync, no waiting. Your layout lives in localStorage until you export it.
